Art direction: porcelain white, muted teal/evergreen, natural pale mint, clean clinical daylight. Elegant humanist Chinese sans-serif with generous line-height, excellent spacing and high contrast. Real photorealistic clinic photography and a friendly Asian female medical service professional in a white coat should be an essential part of the composition, with natural skin texture, softly lit calm contemporary consultation room, wood/linen detail, no CGI. This is service guidance about appointment preparation and clinic visiting, NOT diagnosis.
Distinctive layout: desktop has a full-height softly lit editorial clinic photograph as the left 38% side of the webpage, tasteful brand “和禾健康” and a very restrained headline “安心就诊，从这里开始” over clean light space in the photo. The right 62% is the main porcelain-white chat surface, FLUSH within the page with no outer app-card or card frame. A flat top line with “就诊服务咨询”, small green availability dot and “服务顾问在线”, and a top-right quiet outline button “留下留言”. Thin dividers and typography organize the surface.
